01 • NUST Balochistan Campus Assignment KAREZ_620510.cpp C++

Karez — Quetta Water Distribution Assignment

Source File: KAREZ_620510.cpp • Subterranean Karez Hydraulic & Flow Distribution Model • Quetta, Balochistan

Overview

Implemented in C++ as KAREZ_620510.cpp for my BS Artificial Intelligence coursework at NUST Balochistan Campus (NBC) in Quetta, this assignment models subterranean Karez hydraulic flow, mother-well intake dynamics, sloped tunnel conveyance, and equitable water distribution.

Architecture & Key Highlights

  • C++ Source Implementation (KAREZ_620510.cpp): Structured algorithmic implementation simulating water distribution logic and allocation calculations.
  • Subterranean Hydraulic Model: Represents mother wells, vertical access/ventilation shafts, and gravity-fed tunnel conveyance across Quetta.
  • Equitable Flow Allocation: Evaluates water distribution schedules for sustainable arid-zone groundwater management in Balochistan.
